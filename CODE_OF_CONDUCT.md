# Working together

WLKR.SI project spaces should be useful places to build, learn and ask questions. This guide applies to issues, pull requests and other spaces maintained for our projects. A project's own conduct policy takes precedence.

- Be direct and kind. Discuss the work, explain disagreements and respect other people's time and experience.
- Give people room to learn. Questions, mistakes and constructive criticism are welcome.
- Keep discussions relevant. Do not harass, threaten, demean or repeatedly target someone. Unwanted sexual attention and discriminatory abuse are unacceptable.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information, messages or credentials. Remove sensitive details from examples and logs.
- Respect boundaries. Stop unwanted contact and follow a maintainer's request to move or end a discussion.

## Raise a concern

For a sensitive conduct concern, email [hello@wlkr.si](mailto:hello@wlkr.si), the contact published on [WLKR.SI](https://wlkr.si/about). Include the project, relevant links and a short description. Share only the personal information needed to explain what happened; do not put private reports in public issues.

Maintainers may ask for clarification, remove disruptive content or limit participation in project spaces. Decisions depend on the situation and available evidence. There is no guaranteed response time.

For vulnerabilities or exposed credentials, follow [security reporting](SECURITY.md). For ordinary project questions, use the [support guide](SUPPORT.md).
